# Labs

Phase 2 runs weeks 11–15 and ends with Capstone 1. Each lab has a goal, a brief, concepts, steps naming files and TODOs, a check, evidence and a stretch task.

| Week | Lab | Exercises |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 11 | [LLM APIs, prompts and citations](week-11-llm-apis-and-citations/README.md) | `build_messages`, `parse_citations` |
| 12 | [Ingestion: PDFs, OCR and tables](week-12-ingestion/README.md) | `table_to_markdown`, `chunk_text` |
| 13 | [Retrieval: BM25, dense and hybrid](week-13-retrieval/README.md) | `bm25_idf`, `bm25_tf`, `rrf_fuse` |
| 14 | [Grounding: refusal, tools and MCP](week-14-grounding-tools-mcp/README.md) | `should_refuse`, `compute_ratio` |
| 15 | [Evaluate, serve and present](week-15-evaluate-and-serve/README.md) | `recall_at_k`, `numeric_match`, results table |

`make exercises` lists what is left. The starter's `make test` and CI skip exercise tests, so your build stays green while you work.
